New Waves — About us

About us.

01 — The collective

Bass music between Venice and Pordenone.

New Waves Underground Movement is a bass music collective focused on jungle and drum & bass, active between Venice and Pordenone.

Djambe, Dema, Coce, Marsiattack and Damnspark bring their own sound and visual concept to CS Rivolta in Venice and Astro Club in Pordenone, with plenty of gigs across the region.

Beyond the club scene, the collective organises Mountain Waves Fest, has curated a stage at the Veneto Blaze festival for two years, has an artist residency at Radio Ombra in Venice, and created and hosts Bass Files, a format of interviews and DJ sets dedicated to the scene.

02 — The members · 5 heads, no hierarchy

Five heads.

01

Djambe

DJ — Public relations

Djambe is a DJ based in Venice, active in the Italian bass music scene with a sound that blends jungle, drum and bass, dubstep and UK influences.

Years of exploring clubs, events and sound systems across northern Italy, sharing the stage with names such as Black Sun Empire, QZB and HLZ.

Founder of New Waves Underground Movement. With the collective, Djambe created and hosts Bass Files, a format of interviews and DJ sets in collaboration with Drum And Bass Italia, and looks after the collaboration with Radio Ombra, an independent radio station based at Chet, a historic venue in Venice.

Djambe's sets are always after a line of deep bass and driving rhythms.

Instagram @djambe__

02

Dema

DJ — Audio and lighting tech

Lorenzo Demagistris, known as Dema, started out in the hip-hop scene, organising freestyle contests and having a go at scratching.

Dema then met Bassi Gradassi, a leading sound system of the Venetian reggae and dancehall scene: together they created the Bass Society format, a mix of 140, dubstep and dnb that even took them to Outlook Festival.

Dema then landed at New Waves, organising dark, deep drum and bass nights with the collective. Bass Files was born with the collective: a format in collaboration with Drum And Bass Italia that interviews the best artists of the Italian scene. So was the collaboration with Radio Ombra, an independent radio station based at Chet, a historic venue in Venice.

Dema's sets blend dark sounds and jungle with modern, experimental rhythms.

Instagram @djdema_productions

03

Coce

DJ — Lighting

Coce was born in Trieste in 2000 and taught themself to mix at 14, influenced by '90s–2000s house and by dubstep.

Coce then moved into the dnb-jungle scene, with the aim of bringing it back into the club culture of the city and the surrounding area, as it used to be.

The sound runs across the whole spectrum of the macro-genres, with the aim of bringing something fresh and innovative to clubs.

Today Coce is an integral part of the New Waves collective, sharing its goal of relaunching bass music in Veneto and the wider Triveneto area. The same mission continues with BPM Arena, focused on Trieste and its surroundings.

Instagram @alessandro_coce

04

MRSN

Visual artist

MRSN, formerly Marsiattack, is a founding member of New Waves Underground Movement, and the collective's visual artist and graphic designer.

She designs the posters for the collective and its events and runs the live visuals at its nights.

Her style is abstract: she works with material collected over the years, photos and video, which she breaks down and turns into textures, giving it new life.

05

Damnspark

Visual artist

Damnspark is a founding member of New Waves Underground Movement and the creator of the collective's logo.

Damnspark makes the artwork for Bass Files, the collective's interview and DJ set format in collaboration with Drum And Bass Italia.

Damnspark is also in charge of merchandise and gadgets.
