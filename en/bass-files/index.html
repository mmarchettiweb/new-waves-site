Bass Files.

01 — The format

Interviews and DJ sets on the scene.

×In collaboration with Drum And Bass Italia

Bass Files is the interview and DJ set format dedicated to the Italian drum & bass and jungle scene, created by Djambe and Dema in collaboration with Niko of Drum And Bass Italia.

Each episode is an in-depth interview with an artist, followed by their DJ set: producers play only their own tracks, while those who are a reference point of the scene as selectors bring their own selection.

From the veterans who built the scene to the new generations, Bass Files gives space to people with a story to tell, with no hype and no promotion.

Episodes are available on the Drum And Bass Italia YouTube channel and on the New Waves SoundCloud channel.
