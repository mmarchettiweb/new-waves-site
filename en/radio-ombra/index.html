Radio Ombra.

01 — The residency

Artist residency in Venice.

New Waves' artist residency at Radio Ombra, an independent radio station based at Chet Bar, a historic venue in Venice, is a collective project dedicated to the bass music of the Venetian scene.

One day a month, for two hours, live DJ sets from Chet Bar on Radio Ombra's YouTube channel. The streams then stay available online.

The project is designed to grow: interviews with DJs, producers and promoters from the Venetian scene will be added in future.

Two hours a month with Venetian bass music, from the people who live it and tell its story.
